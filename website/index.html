inua
    A desktop shell

    A quiet Linux Desktop

    inua gives your compositor the rest of a desktop: a bar, a launcher, a control center, notifications, a dock and a settings window. It is written in Go and GTK4, drawn in hairlines and a single accent, and it stays out of the way until you need it.

    It works with Hyprland today.

    InstallAppsSource
    What it is

    The parts of a desktop we touch every day

    Bar
    Workspaces as small squares, the window you are in, the time, and quiet ways into everything else.
    Launcher
    One search for apps and open windows. Escape returns you exactly where you were.
    Control center
    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, power, brightness, sound and media, from the services you already run.
    Notifications
    A brief banner, then a calm list grouped by app. Focus keeps them silent.
    Dock
    The apps you keep and the ones that are open. It can hide until you reach for it.
    Settings
    One window for themes, wallpaper, shortcuts, keyboard and touchpad. Changes apply as you make them.
    How it is made

    Small, plain, and meant to last.

    1. Go, without cgo.GTK4 through puregotk; the whole shell is a single go build.
    2. Layouts are markup.Every surface is a Blueprint file styled from colour tokens. Go only fills in state.
    Colourways

    Seven, from paper to night.

    Install

    One command.

    curl -fsSL https://inua.lyric.im/install.sh | sh
# then log out and choose Inua

    If you already use Hyprland, keep your config and add exec-once = inua-shell and, as the last line, source = ./inua.conf.
