inua
    Apps

    Made for inua, not bound to it.

    Each app is a program of its own. In inua it takes the shell's colours and opens from the launcher and the dock; on another Wayland desktop, it opens all the same.

    The shellInstallSource
    Apps

    The ones you keep open.

    Files
    A file manager, keyboard first: one or two panes, drives, and remote disks through rclone. inua-files
    Terminal
    Terminals for many agents at once, each one's state where you can see it. inua-term
    Mail
    Every account in one list. The assistant helps only when asked. inua-mail
    Disk Usage
    What fills a disk, and which of the folders developers and agents leave behind can go. inua-disk-usage
    Remote Desktop
    Other machines' desktops here, over Moonlight's protocol. inua-remote
    Mobile Link
    Your phone and this computer together, over KDE Connect's protocol. inua-connect
    Virtual machines
    Windows, Linux and FreeBSD guests, each one a folder. inua-vm

    Viewer

    Images, PDFs, Markdown and plain text. Step through a folder with the next images already loaded, zoom and rotate, read a PDF page by page, or a README laid out with its tables and links.

    Light apps

    Small windows that do one thing.

    Twelve of them, quick to open: inua-apps clock, inua-apps weather …

    01

    Clock

    World clocks at a glance, alarms that ring even when Clock is closed, a stopwatch and a timer.

    02

    Weather

    The weather now, for the next 24 hours and the next seven days, for the places you keep. From Open-Meteo, with no account.

    03

    Stocks

    A watchlist with a chart for each, from a day to five years.

    04

    Calculator

    Type or click; the answer is there as you go. Scientific keys when you want them.

    05

    Calendar

    The month with week numbers. Pick a day to see how far it is from today, its week and its day of the year.

    06

    Color Picker

    Pick any colour on the screen with a loupe, then copy it as hex, RGB or HSL. The colours you picked stay at hand.

    07

    System Monitor

    Every core, memory, the GPU, network and disks with their last minute. Open any process to see what it uses, and end it gently, firmly, or with its children.

    08

    Tailscale

    Your tailnet's devices, online first, with their addresses one click away. Ping them, send them files, pick an exit node, and change this device's settings.

    09

    Pass

    Your password store in three columns: categories, entries, and the one you open, with its one-time code and every value a click from the clipboard. When gpg needs your passphrase, inua asks in its own dialog, with the key, what it is for and who asked, and hides them in one click before you share your screen.

    GPG
    Encrypt, sign and decrypt, text or files, with your keyring.
    Music
    A player in Winamp's spirit, with an equalizer and a spectrum.
    Video
    A plain player that remembers where you left each video.
    Games
    Minesweeper, Space Pinball and Small Fortune, offline. inua-games
    Workshop
    Your own light app, in any language, drawn in the shell's look.
    Opening them

    Wherever you reach for them.

    Launcher
    Type a few letters of the name.
    Dock
    The drawer at the end of the dock holds them all.
    Command
    inua-apps clock, inua-apps tailscale …

    Each runs once: opening it again brings its window to you, on the workspace you are on.

    Outside inua

    On another desktop.

    They need a Wayland desktop with GTK 4, and start from your own launcher. Two things wait for the shell: Color Picker's pick from the screen, and sharing this desktop from Remote Desktop.

    The install puts them in your launcher; with INUA_NO_SESSION=1 it leaves your login alone.
